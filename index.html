Pranayama

Come back to your breath.

Explore five traditional breathing practices. Keep the breath comfortable and pause if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable.

Anulom Vilom

Alternate nostril breathing at an easy, even pace. Begin seated comfortably and let the breath stay gentle throughout.

Bhramari

Often called bee breath, Bhramari pairs a relaxed inhale with a soft humming sound on the exhale.

Bhastrika

Known as bellows breath, Bhastrika uses active inhalations and exhalations. Learn this vigorous technique with a qualified instructor.

Ujjayi

A gentle narrowing at the throat gives this breath its soft, ocean-like sound. It is often paired with steady movement in yoga.

Nadi Shodhana

Another name for a family of alternate nostril practices. Traditions vary, and some use specific counts or breath holds. Begin with a comfortable rhythm and seek instruction before adding holds.
